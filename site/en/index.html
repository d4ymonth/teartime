TearTime 한국어

The eye drop reminder
for people who live on their Mac

TearTime counts the time you actually spend at your screen and pops up a gentle reminder when it's time for eye drops. Free, private, and tiny in your menu bar.

Download free (macOS 15+)

Apple silicon and Intel. Read the install note below first.

Features

Screen time based

Pauses when you step away for more than 2 minutes. Pick an interval from 20 to 120 minutes.

Daily limit

Counts today's drops and stops reminding you once you hit your limit (6 by default).

History grid

A GitHub-style grid of the last 20 weeks. Days over your limit turn orange.

One tiny drop icon

Takes almost no menu bar space. One click to log "I just used drops".

5 languages

English, Korean, Japanese, German, Simplified Chinese. Follows your Mac's language.

Private by design

No account, no server. Your data stays on your Mac, and the code is open.

Install

  1. Download TearTime.zip, unzip it, and move TearTime.app to Applications.
  2. On first launch macOS says it cannot verify the app, because it is distributed outside the App Store and not notarized. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  4. A drop icon appears in your menu bar. Click it, then Settings… to set the interval, daily limit and launch at login.

Or, from Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/TearTime.app

FAQ

Is TearTime free?

Yes, and the source code is on GitHub.

macOS says it cannot verify the app.

That warning appears for apps that are not notarized by Apple. Follow step 3 of Install.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. Your history and settings stay on your Mac.

How is this different from a 20-20-20 break timer?

Break timers remind you to look away. TearTime is about eye drops: today's count, a daily limit, and a long-term history.

TearTime is not a medical device and does not replace medical advice. Follow your doctor's or pharmacist's directions for eye drops.
